Dive Deep Into Indonesia.

WAOW 2 was designed around diving.

From the first descent to the last surface interval, every detail is
there to make your time underwater as seamless as possible.

New Sites. Every Day.

On WAOW, the day follows the rhythm of the sea.
A first dive in the morning. Another after breakfast.
One in the afternoon. And one more before the sun
goes down.
Four dives a day means more time underwater, more
sites to discover and more chances to encounter the
extraordinary marine life of Indonesia.
And between them? Good food, warm drinks, a little
rest and plenty of time to enjoy being at sea.

20 guests.
5 guides.
Small groups.

Less People.
More Ocean.

With five experienced dive guides for a
maximum of 20 guests, groups are kept small —
with a maximum ratio of 1 guide to 4 divers.
Small groups mean more freedom in the water,
more personal guidance and more space to
experience each dive at your own pace.
Our guides bring years of experience, a deep
knowledge of the region and, most importantly, a
genuine love for being underwater.

Built for Diving.

Everything has been designed around the dive, from getting to the reef
to getting your gear ready for the next descent.

Dive Centre

A dedicated space for gear preparation, storage and rinsing, with everything ready for your next dive.

Dive Tenders

Multiple tenders keep groups small and make it easy to reach remote
dive sites.

Camera Room

A dedicated space to prepare, organise and take care of your
underwater photo and video equipment between dives.

Dive Longer.
Learn Along the Way.

Make more of your time underwater, or take the opportunity
to learn something new while you're on board.

Nitrox

More time underwater. Nitrox is available on board for certified
divers free of charge. Nitrox matters more on a liveaboard,
with dives stacking up, it eases nitrogen loading and lowers the
risk of DCS. Nitrox training is available on board.

Private PADI Courses

Want to improve your skills, work towards a new certification or
simply feel more confident underwater?
Private PADI instruction is available on board, with one-to-one
guidance tailored to your experience and goals.

Private Guide

For a more personal experience, a private guide can accompany
you underwater.

Our Philosophy.

With more than 30 years of experience in the industry,
our philosophy is built around four simple ideas: explore further, keep groups small,
make diving effortless and give divers the freedom to focus on the experience itself.

Go Further

extraordinary, with the range to travel

Keep It Personal

Make It Effortless

Dive With Confidence

Modern facilities, experienced guides

remote diving operations.
