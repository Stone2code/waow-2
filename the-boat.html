The Boat.

Meet WAOW 2, a 32-meter phinisi built for long days at sea, colorful dives and slow journeys through Indonesia.

Set to set sail October 12th, 2028.

WAOW 2 was built in the classic phinisi tradition, carrying the comfort of a modern yacht and safety held to the highest standard.

The Cabins.

Master Cabin

Our largest cabin, 50 m² with 180° views and a private terrace, your own front-row seat to Indonesia.

Superior Cabins

The Superior Cabins, 20 m², put you close to the action while giving you a comfortable space to retreat to between dives.

Single Cabins

At 13 m², the Single Cabins make the most of their space, with an Upper Deck location and beautiful views out to sea.

Deluxe Cabins

At 22 m², the Deluxe Cabins offer generous space on the Lower Deck, with porthole windows bringing the sea into view.

Outdoor Spaces

A multi-level outdoor lounge offers guests ample space to sit back and let the hours pass by.

The covered communal dining area is the center of the ship — the perfect spot for a specially crafted menu, sourced with local ingredients, under open sky.

Indoor Spaces

The beautiful interiors are a wonderful gathering place while also providing a respite from the equatorial sun.

Featuring a fully air-conditioned communal dining area, seated bar, and a separate seating area with a long sofa perfect for a movie night, the indoor area is an ideal spot to discuss the next adventure.

Made for
Divers.

WAOW 2 was designed from the start as a dive boat, with everything you need between dives, and nothing that gets in the way of them.

A dedicated dive centre, camera room, generous tenders, smart gear storage and safety systems are all designed around a full boat of guests and guides, keeping life on board smooth from the first descent to the last surface interval.
